# Lite-Site Templates

Pre-designed website templates that customers can choose from. Each template is a 2-3 page mobile-friendly site that launches in 48 hours.

## Template 1: "Classic Business" 
**Best for:** Professional services, consultants, contractors

**Pages:**
- Home (hero, services, testimonials, CTA)
- About (company story, team, credentials)
- Contact (form, map, phone/email)

**Features:**
- Clean, professional design
- Blue/gray color scheme
- Service highlights grid
- testimonial carousel
- Google Maps integration
- Contact form with email notifications

**Demo:** `/templates/classic`

---

## Template 2: "Modern Retail"
**Best for:** Shops, restaurants, salons, retail

**Pages:**
